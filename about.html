OUR STORY

A living art, not just a glass box.

FNB AQUATIC STUDIO grew from a simple fascination: the way a small underwater world can change the feeling of an entire room. Today, that fascination guides every project we take on in Chennai.

We bring together the eye of a designer and the patience of a keeper. We listen before we sketch, choose each element with care and build aquariums that feel as if they’ve always belonged.

From a first-time planted tank to a statement installation for a considered space, our promise is the same: honest advice, thoughtful craftsmanship and care that continues long after the water is clear.
